# Hardware guide (for the user, and for our own test builds)

## Materials
- Cardboard (thick, 5 mm+) or a wooden board. Cardboard works for a first try but pins loosen.
- Push pins or small nails. One per pin position (e.g. 200).
- Thread: sewing thread or thin crochet cotton. Black on white board is the classic.
- Printer (optional) for the template. Otherwise a ruler and a protractor.

## Steps
1. Print the pin template from the app and tape it on the board, or mark pins by measurement.
2. Push a pin into every numbered dot. Keep them straight and the same height.
3. Tie the thread to pin 1.
4. Follow the sequence in the app: go to the next number, wrap once around the pin, continue.
5. Tie off at the last pin.

## Things to test ourselves
- How many pins fit on a 30 cm circle before they are too close to wrap? (Spacing ≈ 0.47 cm at 200 pins.)
- Does cardboard hold 200 pins under thread tension?
