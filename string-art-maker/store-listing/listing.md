# Play Store listing (draft)

**Title (30 chars max):** String Art Maker

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Turn any photo into a pin-and-thread art guide. Numbers tell you where to go.

**Full description (draft):**
Pick a photo. Choose a circle or square board and how many pins you have. String Art Maker gives you a printable pin template and a numbered thread sequence. Follow the numbers with one long thread and watch the picture appear on your board.

- Works offline, nothing is uploaded
- Circle and square boards
- Printable to-scale pin template, or measurements if you have no printer
- Step-by-step mode with big numbers and saved progress
- Export the sequence as text or PDF

**Category:** Art & Design
